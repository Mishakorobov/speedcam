Final design

Velocity Iris

An aerodynamic 3-blade aperture shutter fusing speed turbine dynamics with a precision camera lens pupil.

  • Camera & Radar integration: three rotational swept vanes evoke both an optical aperture shutter and a dynamic tachometer turbine.
  • Uncompromising clarity at 16 px: bold, clean geometry and 12-unit channels ensure zero loss of legibility in status bars and smartwatches.
  • Automotive presence: Electric Blaze Orange (#FF5E1E) against Carbon Slate delivers instant warning recognition without visual fatigue.
