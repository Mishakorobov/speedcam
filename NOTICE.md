# Third-party notices

## OpenStreetMap

Camera position, type, direction and speed-limit data are derived from OpenStreetMap
and are licensed under the **Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**.

Required attribution, which must be displayed wherever camera data is presented:

> Data © OpenStreetMap contributors

ODbL imposes share-alike on derived databases: if a substantially-derived database is
redistributed, it must be released under ODbL terms. Personal, non-distributed use is
unaffected.

- Licence text: <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>
- Tag definitions: `highway=speed_camera`, `type=enforcement`

## SpeedCam community corpus

Camera data from the SpeedCam-format community corpus is **not yet cleared for use**.
The format is public and widely distributed, but the terms governing the particular
export used have not been verified. This is tracked as a blocking risk in `../Plan.md`
§9.1 and must be resolved before the importer is written.
